Gemini Rue 简体中文汉化补丁 / Simplified Chinese Localization Patch
====================================================================

安装 (Install)
--------------
1. 把本压缩包内的 5 个游戏文件全部解压到游戏根目录
   (Steam\steamapps\common\Gemini Rue\, 即 Gemini Rue.exe 旁边):
     SChinese.tra
     agsfnt3.ttf  agsfnt4.ttf  agsfnt5.ttf  agsfnt6.ttf
2. 用记事本打开游戏目录下的 .config 文件, 把
     translation=German,Polish
   改成
     translation=German,Polish,SChinese
   保存。(这一步让语言出现在设置菜单里)
3. 在 Steam 点"开始游戏", 启动弹窗里选 Settings / 游戏设置
   (或直接双击游戏目录里的 winsetup.exe),
   在语言下拉框选 SChinese, 保存。
4. 启动游戏, 即为中文。

   备选: 跳过第 2-3 步, 直接用记事本打开 acsetup.cfg,
   在 [language] 段写入 translation=SChinese, 效果相同。

   English: extract the 5 files next to "Gemini Rue.exe"; add SChinese
   to the translation= list in ".config"; hit Play in Steam and choose
   Settings (= winsetup.exe), pick SChinese in the language dropdown,
   save, then launch. (Or just set translation=SChinese under
   [language] in acsetup.cfg.)

适用版本 (Supported version)
----------------------------
Steam 2026 (AGS 3.6.1) 重制构建。旧 GOG/实体版不支持。
Steam 2026 rebuild (AGS 3.6.1). Older GOG/retail builds unsupported.

卸载 (Uninstall)
----------------
删除上述 5 个文件, 并把语言设置改回原样。存档不受影响。
Delete the 5 files and revert the language setting. Saves unaffected.
