Gemini Rue 简体中文汉化补丁 / Simplified Chinese Localization Patch
====================================================================

安装 (Install)
--------------
1. 把本压缩包内的 5 个游戏文件全部解压到游戏根目录
   (Steam\steamapps\common\Gemini Rue\, 即 Gemini Rue.exe 旁边):
     SChinese.tra
     agsfnt3.ttf  agsfnt4.ttf  agsfnt5.ttf  agsfnt6.ttf
2. 打开游戏目录下的 acsetup.cfg, 在 [language] 段写入:
     translation=SChinese
   (或把 .config 里的 translation=German,Polish 改成
    translation=German,Polish,SChinese, 然后在 winsetup.exe 里选 SChinese)
3. 启动游戏。

   Extract the 5 game files above into the game root next to
   "Gemini Rue.exe", set "translation=SChinese" under [language] in
   acsetup.cfg (or add SChinese to the .config translation list and pick
   it in winsetup.exe), then launch the game.

适用版本 (Supported version)
----------------------------
Steam 2026 (AGS 3.6.1) 重制构建。旧 GOG/实体版不支持。
Steam 2026 rebuild (AGS 3.6.1). Older GOG/retail builds unsupported.

卸载 (Uninstall)
----------------
删除上述 5 个文件, 并把 acsetup.cfg 的 translation= 改回空。存档不受影响。
Delete the 5 files and clear translation= in acsetup.cfg. Saves unaffected.
